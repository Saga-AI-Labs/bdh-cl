The gate is the one registered in 8.2: for each dimension, `mean_d >= M_d` and
`min_d >= F_d`, with no mean across dimensions. `F_d` is the negative-band
maximum plus one; `F_d_strict` is the positive-band minimum and is recorded only
as the conservative alternative. `M_d` is the positive-band mean minus 0.5.

Margin sensitivity, for the record: at 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7, `M_d` is 4.70 / 4.50 /
4.30 on coherence and relevance, and 4.53 / 4.33 / 4.13 on language.

The margin is a strictness choice for the host verdict, not a quantity the
calibration measures. The bands are four points apart on coherence and relevance
and two on language, so any margin below 4.0 satisfies the positive band and
rejects the negative band on coherence and relevance; the margin therefore
cannot be read off the reference set. 0.5 is the registered candidate (8.3) and
is kept because it leaves `M_d` half a point under a positive band at ceiling
while staying at least 2.8 above every negative-band mean. The scale-set controls
(`long_wrong`, `planted_error`, `audience_violation`, 3.82-3.89 on coherence)
are **not** the reference for `M_d`: they are flawed texts by construction and
would fail a 4.50 coherence gate, which is correct. The acceptance check that
requires them to stay at or above coherence 3 is a check on the judge, not a
statement that they should pass the host gate.

The gate prompt split is unchanged from 8.3: 133 prompts, of which 100 gate
(50 explain + 50 describe) and 33 argue diagnostic. Gate statistics use the 100;
the 33 are reported separately.

### 4.1 The fluent-wrong check does not pass as written

The criterion is `language >= 4` and `coherence >= 3` for `bad`, `long_wrong`,
`planted_error` and `audience_violation`. Language passes everywhere (min 4).
Coherence passes on every variant mean, but six of 71 comparable cells fall
below 3:

| item | score | why |
|---|---|---|
| `cert_en17_long_wrong` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en03_planted_error` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en04_planted_error` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en09_audience_violation` | 2 | list whose points contradict the prompt's premise |
| `cert_en14_audience_violation` | 1 | pseudo-legal word salad, no recoverable proposition |
| `cert_en17_audience_violation` | 1 | pseudo-legal word salad, no recoverable proposition |

Two of these are the check being wrong rather than the judge: under v2 anchor 1,
text with no recoverable proposition scores 1 even when it is grammatical, and
`cert_en14`/`en17_audience_violation` are dense invented legal terminology with
no recoverable claim. Gemma scores `cert_en17_audience_violation` 5, which is the
register bias the operator ruling of 2026-11-23 removed; Qwen scores it 1.

The check also failed under v1 (`planted_error` min 2, `audience_violation` min
1), so this is not a v2 regression - it is an acceptance line that was never
reconciled with the anchors. Three readings are open:

- (a) read the check as a per-variant mean. It passes, but this hides individual
  failures and contradicts the per-item floor the rest of the gate relies on.
- (b) **adopted**: amend the check so it applies to items whose intended
  defect is not one the rubric scores. Draft wording: "the fluent-wrong controls
  are checked for `language >= 4`, and for `coherence >= 3` only where the
  variant's intended defect is neither an internal contradiction nor an
  unrecoverable proposition; contradiction and word-salad controls are reported
  separately." This keeps the check doing its real job - catching a judge that
  imports 4.4b correctness into key 2 - without asking it to reject texts the v2
  anchors place at 1-2 by design.
- (c) hold the freeze. Not warranted: none of the six cells is in the positive
  or negative calibration band, so the thresholds do not move.

Option (b) does not clear every cell. `cert_en09_audience_violation` (2, both
seats at or below 3) is a format and relevance defect - four reasons where the
instruction asks for two, one of them off-topic - not a contradiction and not
word salad. Under v2 the coherence score of 2 is arguably a format leak, and it
survives exemption (b).

**Recorded exception (operator, 2026-11-24).** This cell is accepted as
non-blocking and is named here so the frozen record does not claim a clean pass
on the fluent-wrong check. It is not evidence that the threshold is wrong: it is
one item where a format defect moved the coherence score, and it sits outside
both calibration bands. The exemption wording signed with this freeze is the
draft above, applied with `cert_en09_audience_violation` carried as the single
named exception.
