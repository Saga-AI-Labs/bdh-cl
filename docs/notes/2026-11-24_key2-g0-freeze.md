### 4.1 The fluent-wrong check does not pass as written

The criterion is `language >= 4` and `coherence >= 3` for `bad`, `long_wrong`,
`planted_error` and `audience_violation`. Language passes everywhere (min 4).
Coherence passes on every variant mean, but six of 71 comparable cells fall
below 3:

| item | score | why |
|---|---|---|
| `cert_en17_long_wrong` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en03_planted_error` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en04_planted_error` | 2 | planted contradiction that damages the main proposition |
| `cert_en09_audience_violation` | 2 | list whose points contradict the prompt's premise |
| `cert_en14_audience_violation` | 1 | pseudo-legal word salad, no recoverable proposition |
| `cert_en17_audience_violation` | 1 | pseudo-legal word salad, no recoverable proposition |

Two of these are the check being wrong rather than the judge: under v2 anchor 1,
text with no recoverable proposition scores 1 even when it is grammatical, and
`cert_en14`/`en17_audience_violation` are dense invented legal terminology with
no recoverable claim. Gemma scores `cert_en17_audience_violation` 5, which is the
register bias the operator ruling of 2026-11-23 removed; Qwen scores it 1.

The check also failed under v1 (`planted_error` min 2, `audience_violation` min
1), so this is not a v2 regression - it is an acceptance line that was never
reconciled with the anchors. The operator has to choose one of:

- (a) read the check as a per-variant mean, in which case it passes;
- (b) amend the check to exempt items that are word salad by construction, in
  which case it passes;
- (c) hold the freeze.

The candidate thresholds do not depend on the outcome: none of the six cells is
in the positive or negative calibration band.

2. **Language agreement fails on exact, passes on +/-1.** 69.2 % exact /

   registered for language, but the exact-agreement gate is not met.
3. **One not-evaluable cell** in the scale set (section 4), 0.9 %, under cap.
4. **The human read has not been done.** The 8.3 requirement that the operator
   read 10 worst and 10 random samples blind is open. The ChatGPT read is
   explicitly not a substitute and is labelled as calibration in section 9 of the
   gate-failure note.

Relevance is the one dimension that passes the pair gate outright: 82.2 % exact /
96.3 % +/-1 on the scale set, 95.8 % / 100 % on the control set.
