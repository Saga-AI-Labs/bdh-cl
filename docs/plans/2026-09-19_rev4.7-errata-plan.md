## 0. Why a revision, and why 4.7

Because of one asymmetry, not because of a hit rate. Of seventeen claims, ten hold, four hold in part,
two do not hold as stated, and one holds in substance on a number the audit itself mis-printed. The
reason to revise is that the single most consequential finding costs almost nothing to fix and is the
most usable against us: a `[PROVED]` stamp on an unproved sentence that is the only bridge from the
exact theory to the measured results section.

4.7 rather than 5: nearly everything lies inside the existing structure, with no new experiments
required for the majority. The git history already has the category for this kind of commit
(`Rev 4.6 Tier 0: formulation fixes from the audit, no measurements changed`). And "rev 5" would
claim new results, which is exactly what the revision must not claim.

## 1. Method

Each claim was decided in two separate parts — *citation* (does the manuscript say what the audit
says it says) and *computation* (is the audit's own arithmetic right) — against primary sources: the
TeX verbatim, `docs/reports/data/` and `docs/data/` for measured values, and a CAS-free recomputation
script (`out_c/union_alpha_audit/check_manuscript_audit.py`) for every arithmetic claim. The
manuscript is the object under attack, so no quoted number was checked against the manuscript's own
prose.

One error in our own first pass is recorded here because it changed the plan: the review initially
claimed the forgetting matrix had no artifact. It does — `docs/reports/data/2026-09-10_fcs_matrix.csv`.
The correction is in §C.7 of the review; the plan below is built on the corrected reading.

## 3. What the revision must not inherit

Applying the audit directly would import the auditor's own errors. The revision applies the *review*,
not the raw audit. Three audit errors must not enter the manuscript:

1. **"four decimal places"** (A.2): the subtraction gives three. Our own fix prints "four" about the
   cross-instrument agreement, not about the two printed values — those agree to three, which the
   body already says.
2. **the refuted universal negative** (D.13): our sentence already carries its scope limit; do not
   weaken it further to satisfy a charge that reads a sentence we did not write.
3. **the contraction premise** (B.6): do not add a definition that is already present twice.

## 4. Verification

Before the revision commit:

- every edited line diffs against the intended change only;
- `prop:soft` `=1` re-checked against the appendix derivation;
- the Tier-1 edits re-read once in the manuscript, not only in the diff;
- PDF rebuilds and the `.md` regenerates from the `.tex` so the three files stay one revision;
- the number-against-artifact check (below) re-run on the touched claims.

## 5. Open question carried into the revision

The audit found the same pattern six times: a figure asserted and never recomputed. Whether it extends
further is untested. A number-against-artifact sweep of the manuscript is the honest precondition for
"publishable" and is the natural Tier 4 — not scheduled here, and the first draft of our own review is
the evidence for how easy it is to be wrong doing exactly that.
